# Mock-interview avatars

`emma-v1.glb` (Emma Clarke) and `daniel-v1.glb` (Daniel Okafor) were built with
Blender 4.5 LTS and MPFB 2.0.17 from MakeHuman assets released under CC0
(MakeHuman system assets, Skins 01/02, Suits 01 by Margaret Toigo, Visemes 02,
Faceunits 01 — https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html),
rigged with TalkingHead's MIT-licensed custom rig, then compressed with
glTF-Transform (meshopt + WebP). Rebuild with `scripts/avatars/README.md`.
