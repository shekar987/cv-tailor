About Stackfield Technologies

Stackfield Technologies is a growing fintech startup building payment infrastructure for small and medium businesses across the UK. We're a team of 45 engineers who care deeply about code quality, honest communication, and shipping things that work.

The role

We're looking for a Junior Software Engineer to join our platform team. You'll work alongside senior engineers on our Django-based backend services, pick up real responsibilities from day one, and grow quickly in a team that invests in its people. This is a great opportunity for a recent graduate or someone a year or two into their career who wants to build production software that processes real transactions.

What you'll be doing

- Building and maintaining REST API endpoints using Python and Django
- Writing unit and integration tests with pytest
- Reviewing pull requests and giving constructive feedback
- Working with PostgreSQL to design and query relational data
- Participating in sprint planning and retrospectives
- Debugging issues in production with the support of senior colleagues

What we're looking for

Required:
- Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field — or equivalent practical experience
- Working knowledge of Python, including standard library usage
- Understanding of relational databases and SQL
- Familiarity with version control using Git
- Good written communication skills and a willingness to ask questions

Nice to have:
- Experience with Django or any other Python web framework
- Exposure to Docker or containerised development environments
- Any personal or university projects that demonstrate software you've built

We actively welcome recent graduates and candidates who are earlier in their careers. No years-of-experience requirement: we care more about curiosity and rigour than a long CV.

What we offer

- £38,000 – £45,000 depending on experience
- 25 days holiday plus bank holidays
- Flexible hybrid working (London office, Southwark)
- Personal learning budget of £800 per year
- Private health cover after probation
- Monthly team socials and a no-nonsense engineering culture

We're committed to building a diverse and inclusive team. We encourage applications from candidates of all backgrounds.

To apply, send your CV and a short covering note explaining what interests you about this role to careers@stackfield.example.com.
