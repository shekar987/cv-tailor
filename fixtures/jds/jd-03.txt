About Crestline Systems

Crestline Systems builds infrastructure tooling and monitoring platforms for cloud-native engineering teams. We're 30 people based in Edinburgh, with a remote-first culture that still loves getting together in person. Our customers are platform engineering teams at mid-size SaaS companies who need visibility and reliability at scale.

The role

We're growing our backend team and looking for a Backend Engineer with roughly two years of professional software development experience, ideally with Go at the centre of your work. You'll join a team of four engineers building the data-ingestion and alerting pipelines that power our monitoring platform. The work is technically challenging — high throughput, low latency, correctness under failure — and you'll have real influence over how we build.

Responsibilities

- Designing and implementing Go services for data ingestion and processing
- Writing and maintaining integration tests with real infrastructure (PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka)
- Reviewing your colleagues' code and offering thoughtful feedback
- Contributing to architectural decisions in a team that makes them collaboratively
- Improving observability through structured logging, metrics and tracing
- Responding to on-call incidents on a rotating schedule (approximately one week in four)

What you'll need

Required:
- Around 2 years of experience writing backend services in a production environment
- Working knowledge of Go — you don't need to be an expert, but you should be comfortable with goroutines, channels, and the standard library
- Understanding of relational databases and SQL, particularly PostgreSQL
- Familiarity with containerised environments (Docker, Kubernetes basics)
- Good instincts for reliability: you think about failure modes before they happen

Desirable:
- Experience with message queues or streaming platforms (Kafka, NATS, RabbitMQ)
- Exposure to distributed systems concepts (consensus, consistency, partitioning)
- Any open-source contributions or personal projects in Go

About 2 years of professional experience is what we typically look for, though we care more about the work you've done than the specific number.

What we offer

- £42,000 – £52,000 based on experience
- 25 days annual leave plus Scottish bank holidays
- Flexible working arrangements — most of the team work from the Edinburgh office three days a week
- £1,000 annual training and conference budget
- Private dental and medical insurance
- Meaningful equity options in a company that's growing sustainably

Crestline Systems welcomes applications from engineers at all stages and backgrounds. We review every application and will give you honest, timely feedback.
