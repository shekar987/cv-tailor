About Orbis Payments

Orbis Payments is a licensed payment institution processing card-not-present transactions for e-commerce merchants across the EU and UK. We handle approximately £800m in transaction volume annually and our infrastructure is built for reliability at scale. The engineering team of 22 is growing as we expand into new markets and payment methods.

The role

We're hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to join our transaction processing team. This is a senior-level position, and we mean that: we expect you to bring 5 or more years of professional backend engineering experience and to operate with significant autonomy from day one. You'll be owning complex, high-stakes parts of our payment processing pipeline, contributing to architectural decisions, and helping grow the engineers around you. If you're a mid-level engineer looking to stretch into seniority, this isn't the right role — we'd encourage you to apply again in a year or two.

What we're looking for

Required:
- 5+ years of professional experience as a backend software engineer
- Strong proficiency in at least one of: Go, Rust, Java, Scala (we use Go and Java)
- Demonstrable experience designing and building distributed systems
- Deep understanding of database performance — query plans, indexes, locking behaviour (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Comfortable with event-driven architecture and message queues (we use Kafka)
- Track record of mentoring more junior engineers
- Experience shipping and operating services in a cloud environment (we use AWS)

Desirable:
- Background in payments, fintech, or financial services
- Familiarity with PCI-DSS requirements and secure engineering practices
- Experience with observability tooling (Datadog, OpenTelemetry)
- Participation in architectural review processes

What you'll be doing

- Designing and delivering complex features in our Go and Java payment processing services
- Setting technical direction on the team and writing engineering proposals for significant changes
- Reviewing code, raising standards and mentoring mid-level engineers
- Participating in incident response on a rotating on-call schedule
- Working with product and compliance teams on new payment method integrations
- Contributing to hiring: reviewing CVs, designing technical exercises, running interviews

This role is senior — please only apply if you have 5+ years of genuine backend engineering experience.
