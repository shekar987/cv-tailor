About Halcyon Data

Halcyon Data provides data quality and lineage tooling for financial services firms. Our platform integrates with data warehouses and BI tools to track how data moves, transforms and degrades across an organisation's systems. We're 35 people, based in London, and we've been growing at around 40% year on year for the past three years.

The role

We're looking for a Software Engineer to join our integration team, which is responsible for the connectors that link our platform to third-party data warehouses, pipelines and BI tools. The team builds in Python and TypeScript and you'll be expected to contribute to both. You'll work closely with customer success when new integrations are requested and with the platform team when the connectors surface issues deeper in the system.

Responsibilities

- Building and maintaining integration connectors in Python and TypeScript
- Writing and maintaining automated tests (pytest, Jest)
- Reviewing and triaging integration-related bug reports from the support queue
- Collaborating with the platform team on shared API contracts
- Documenting integration specifications for internal and customer use
- Participating in code review across the team

Requirements

Essential:
- Comfortable working in Python in a production software environment
- Some experience with TypeScript or JavaScript
- Familiarity with REST APIs — consuming and building them
- Solid understanding of relational databases and SQL
- Good written communication skills; some of this role involves writing for a technical audience

Desirable:
- Experience integrating with data warehouse platforms (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift)
- Background in a data or analytics engineering context
- Familiarity with dbt or Airflow
- Experience working in a customer-facing capacity
