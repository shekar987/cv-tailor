About Graymoor Solutions

Graymoor Solutions is an ISV building case management software for local government and housing associations. Our platform is used by 47 councils across England and Wales to manage planning applications, housing repair requests and complaints. We're 90 people, with engineering as the largest function. We've been profitable every year since 2009.

The role

We're hiring a Senior C# .NET Developer to work on our core backend platform. C# is the language our entire backend is written in and has been for 12 years; we're not in the process of migrating to anything else. The right candidate will be comfortable owning complex pieces of the codebase, mentoring more junior engineers, and contributing to technical strategy. This is a senior-level position and we expect candidates to demonstrate that in their work and how they talk about it.

Seniority

This is a senior engineering position. We expect you to demonstrate experience leading technical decisions, owning the design of non-trivial features end-to-end, and helping less experienced colleagues improve. Titles aren't everything — but we're genuinely looking for someone who operates at that level, not someone aspiring to it.

We expect this person to have meaningful seniority — typically that means 5+ years of .NET/C# development, though we care more about the quality of the experience than the number. If you've got three years but have built genuinely complex things, we'd still encourage you to apply.
