About Pemberton Capital Partners

Pemberton Capital Partners is an alternative asset management firm based in Mayfair. We manage £4.2bn across private credit and direct lending strategies. Our technology team of 12 builds and maintains the internal systems that support deal origination, portfolio monitoring and investor reporting.

The engagement

We're looking for an experienced Software Engineer on a contract basis to join our technology team for an initial 6-month engagement. The inside IR35 status has been determined by our IR35 assessment process and will be enforced through an approved umbrella company — we do not engage contractors on a PAYE basis directly or through personal service companies outside this arrangement. The day rate is £400–£500 depending on experience; the engagement will be invoiced through the umbrella company only.

Day rate: £400 – £500 (inside IR35, through umbrella company)

The work

You'll be joining the team during a significant infrastructure modernisation. We're migrating internal reporting systems from an on-premise SQL Server stack to a cloud-native architecture on Azure. The initial contract will focus on data pipeline development and API work, with the possibility of extension.

You will:

- Build and maintain Azure Data Factory pipelines for financial data
- Develop Python-based microservices deployed on Azure Kubernetes Service
- Write and review SQL queries and stored procedures against SQL Server and Azure SQL
- Support integration testing for migrated components
- Document technical decisions in architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Work closely with the technology lead and one permanent engineer on the team

What we need

- Strong Python development experience in a production environment
- Working knowledge of Microsoft Azure (ADF, AKS, Azure SQL, Blob Storage)
- Comfortable with SQL Server / T-SQL
- Experience with containerised deployment and CI/CD pipelines
- Availability to start within 2 weeks
- Willingness to work inside IR35 through an approved umbrella company

Nice to have:

- Financial services or asset management background
- Experience with PowerBI or similar BI tooling
- Knowledge of SSIS or other legacy ETL tooling (useful during migration)

Working arrangement

Hybrid — 3 days on-site at our Mayfair office, 2 days remote. Office hours are 9am–5:30pm; some flexibility is available for the right candidate. All equipment provided.
